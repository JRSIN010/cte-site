This site is run by {{company}} ("we", "us"). This page tells you what we collect and why. We keep it short and plain.

## What we collect

- **Your email address,** if you sign up for emails or a free download.
- **Your name, email and message,** if you use the contact form.
- **Basic visit numbers,** like which pages get read. These do not tell us who you are.

We do not sell your information. We do not ask for more than we need.

## How we use it

- To send you the emails or downloads you asked for.
- To answer your message.
- To see which pages are useful so we can make the site better.

You can stop the emails at any time. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

## Other companies that help us run the site

Some tools we use are run by other companies. They handle your information under their own privacy policies.

- **Email service:** stores your email address and sends our emails.
- **YouTube (Google):** videos on this site come from YouTube. A video only loads after you click play. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
- **Amazon and our book store:** if you click a buy button, you leave this site. The store handles your order and payment. We never see your card number.
- **Web host:** our host may log technical details like your IP address to deliver the pages.
