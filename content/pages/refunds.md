## Books bought on Amazon

Amazon handles your order. Returns and refunds follow Amazon's own rules. Please ask Amazon for help with those orders.

## eBooks bought from our store

Because an eBook is delivered right away, all sales are final once the file is downloaded. But we want you to be happy:

- If the file is broken or will not open, email us and we will fix it or refund you.
- If you bought the same book twice by mistake, email us and we will refund the extra copy.
- Ask within 14 days of your order.

Email {{email}} with your order number and what went wrong. We answer within 3 business days.
