## This is for learning and discussion

Everything here is for education and entertainment. It is not legal, medical, financial or other professional advice.

## A theory is not a fact

We explain theories. Explaining a theory does not mean we say it is true. When something is a guess, we say so. When something is proven, we point to the source.

## We are not accusing anyone

We report what records, courts, officials and witnesses have said. Nothing on this site says that any living person committed a crime unless a court has found so.

## Opinions

Opinions on this site belong to the author. They are not statements of fact.

## About the books

Some books use made-up composite characters to tell a true story more clearly. When a book does this, an author's note inside the book says so.

## Mistakes and corrections

We try hard to be right. If you see a mistake, [tell us](page:contact). We will check it, fix it and note the fix.

## Other websites

We link to other sites and to original documents. We do not control those sites.
