## What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser. It helps a site remember things.

## What this site does

- **This site does not set tracking cookies of its own.**
- **Videos:** a YouTube video loads only after you click play. YouTube may then set its own cookies. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode to keep this low.
- **Forms and stores:** if you sign up for emails or click a buy button, the email service or store may use cookies on its own pages.
- **Visit numbers:** if we count visits, we use a tool that does not need cookies.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work.
