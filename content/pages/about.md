## What this is

Conspiracy Theories Explained looks at famous mysteries and cover-ups. Each one gets a book, and each book gets videos and Shorts on YouTube.

I do not tell you what to believe. I show you the theory, the evidence and both sides. Then you decide.

## How every case file works

1. **The Theory.** What do people say happened? I tell it the way its fans tell it.
2. **The Evidence.** What do the papers, tapes and witnesses show? I point to the source so you can check it.
3. **Both Sides.** What is the best case for it? What is the best case against it?
4. **You Decide.** I stop there. The last step is yours.

## My rules

- I use sources you can check.
- A guess is always marked as a guess.
- I show both sides and I do not pick one.
- If I get a fact wrong, I fix it and say so. [Tell me here](page:contact).

## Who is JR Korvus?

JR Korvus is an investigator of forgotten stories, hidden histories, and enduring mysteries.

His books wander through the spaces where documented history, ancient traditions, folklore, intelligence records, archaeology, and unanswered questions intersect. Rather than offering final answers, he invites readers to explore evidence, challenge assumptions, and decide for themselves.

Some readers say his stories feel as though they were told by someone who quietly watched history unfold from the sidelines, never changing its course. Only remembering what others overlooked.

Whether that's true is a mystery of its own.
