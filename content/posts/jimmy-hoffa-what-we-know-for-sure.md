There are dozens of stories about what happened to Jimmy Hoffa. Before we look at any of them, let's pin down what is solid.

## The short list of facts

- Jimmy Hoffa was the former boss of the Teamsters, one of the biggest unions in America.
- He was last seen on **July 30, 1975**, outside a restaurant near Detroit, Michigan.
- He was 62 years old.
- He was declared legally dead in 1982.
- The FBI has searched many places over the years. No body has been found.
- No one has ever been charged with his disappearance or death.

That is it. Six lines. Everything else is a theory, a tip or a claim.

## Why this case will not go away

A man that famous does not just vanish. Someone knew where he was going that day. Someone knew what happened next.

For fifty years, people have come forward to say they know the answer. Each time, agents dig. Each time, they find nothing.
