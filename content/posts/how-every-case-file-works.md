Most shows about conspiracy theories want you to pick a team. Believe it all, or laugh at it all.

I think that is the wrong game. A better game is to slow down and look.

So every case file on this site, and every book and video, follows the same four steps.

## 1. The Theory

First, I tell the theory the way the people who believe it tell it. No eye rolls. If I cannot explain it fairly, I have not earned the right to judge it.

## 2. The Evidence

Next come the records. Reports, memos, photos, tapes, sworn words. I link to the source when I can, so you can read it yourself. If something is a guess, I label it as a guess.

## 3. Both Sides

Then I give the best case for the theory and the best case against it. Not a weak version of either one. The strongest version.

## 4. You Decide

And then I stop. I do not hand you an answer. You have a brain and you get to use it.

## Why do it this way?

Because real cover-ups have happened. And because some wild stories are just stories. The only way to tell them apart is to check.
