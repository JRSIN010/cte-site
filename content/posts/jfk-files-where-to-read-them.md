People argue about the JFK files all day long. Very few of them have read one.

You can. The files are public and free. Here is where to look.

## The big picture

- President John F. Kennedy was shot in Dallas on **November 22, 1963**.
- In 1964, the Warren Commission said Lee Harvey Oswald acted alone.
- In 1992, Congress passed a law to gather the records in one place and open them to the public.
- On **March 18, 2025**, the National Archives began posting records that had been held back. More than 80,000 pages went up in about a day.

The whole collection holds more than six million pages. Most of it was already open before 2025.

## Where to read

- [The National Archives JFK page](https://www.archives.gov/jfk). This is the front door.
- [The 2025 release](https://www.archives.gov/research/jfk/release-2025). The newest files, as PDFs.
- [The Mary Ferrell Foundation](https://www.maryferrell.org/). A free library that lets you search many of the files by word.

## How to read a file without getting lost

1. **Check the date.** Was it written before or after the event?
2. **Check who wrote it and who got it.** A memo to the boss is not the same as a note to self.
3. **Look at what is blacked out.** Then ask why.
4. **Read the whole page.** One line out of place can change the meaning.

## Then what?

Then you compare. What does the official story say? What do the papers say? Where do they match, and where do they not?
