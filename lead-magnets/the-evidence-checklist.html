The Evidence Checklist

10 questions to ask before you believe a theory, or throw it out.

FREE COPY

Use this on any claim, from any side. Tick a box when you have a real answer. The more boxes you tick, the more you know.

  1. Who is making the claim, and how do they know?Were they there? Did they read the record? Or did they hear it from someone else?
  2. Can I see the source myself?A real source has a name, a date and a place you can find it. "People say" is not a source.
  3. Is this a fact, a guess or an opinion?All three are fine. Mixing them up is the problem.
  4. What would prove it wrong?If nothing could ever prove it wrong, be careful. Good claims can be tested.
  5. What does the other side say, at its best?Find the smartest person who disagrees. Read them, not the weakest one.
  6. Who gains if I believe this?Money, power, clicks, votes. A reason to lie does not prove a lie, but it matters.
  7. Is there a simpler answer?Mistakes, luck and sloppy work explain a lot. Rule them out first.
  8. Do separate sources agree?Ten sites that copy one post are one source. Look for people who did their own work.
  9. What is still unknown?Write down the gaps. "We do not know yet" is an honest answer.
  10. Would I change my mind?If new proof came out tomorrow, would I move? If not, I am defending a team, not looking for the truth.
